Bubble Bloom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Developer: devfoxmy (youssef1590@gmail.com).

Bubble Bloom is a free offline puzzle game. We do not require an account and we do not collect your name, email or contacts.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location, IP address and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, according to Google's Privacy Policy. Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice from Settings.

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, settings and best score are saved only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13.
